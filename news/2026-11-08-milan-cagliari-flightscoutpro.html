2. Statutory Passenger Compensation Under EC 261/2004

Comprehensive EU Rights Summary (Regulation EC No 261/2004)

Operating entirely within European airspace and covering approximately 700 kilometers, the Milan to Cagliari sector falls strictly within Tier 1 Short-Haul Flights (less than 1,500 kilometers) governed by European Regulation (EC) No 261/2004:

  • Cash Compensation: A verifiable flight delay of 3 hours or greater at final arrival in Cagliari triggers a fixed €250 financial compensation per booked traveler, provided delays did not stem from extraordinary meteorological disruption or air traffic management strikes.
  • Cancellation Notice Rules: Cancellations communicated fewer than 14 days prior to departure obligate carriers to provide either a full ticket refund or prompt re-routing, plus applicable statutory compensation.
  • Care Provisions: Delays surpassing two hours require carriers to deliver meal vouchers, refreshments, and communications access directly within the boarding terminal area.
