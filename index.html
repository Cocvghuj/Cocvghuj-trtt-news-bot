KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

Flight delayed
or cancelled?

Under EU Regulation EC 261/2004, eligible passengers may be entitled to assistance, a refund or compensation of up to €600, depending on the circumstances and flight distance.

Check your passenger rights ↗

Keep these details

  • Booking confirmation and reference
  • Boarding pass or e-ticket
  • Actual arrival time and airline notices
  • Receipts for reasonable expenses
Eligibility is assessed case by case. Extraordinary circumstances and other legal conditions may apply.
