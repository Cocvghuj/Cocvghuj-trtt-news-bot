**Applies to:** New posts only. This checklist does not authorize editing or migrating any existing post.

## 1. Required post fields

Before publishing every new post, verify each item:

- [ ] **Title:** clear, accurate, human-readable, and relevant to the story.
- [ ] **Label:** choose the correct existing TRTT category label; use a new label only when the publisher intends to create that category.
- [ ] **Permalink:** short, readable, lowercase, hyphen-separated, stable, and free of spaces or unnecessary dates.
- [ ] **Search Description:** a unique, accurate summary of the article; avoid promises the article does not fulfill.
- [ ] **Image:** use an image the publisher owns or is authorized to use; verify the actual image URL opens.
- [ ] **Image Caption:** describe what the photograph actually shows and add date/location only when known. Do not invent photo details.
- [ ] **Image Alt Text:** concise description for readers using assistive technology; do not keyword-stuff.
- [ ] **Disclosure:** include the approved disclosure when affiliate relationships/qualifying links are present, and keep it visible to readers.
- [ ] **Credit:** include the approved MAC project credit where appropriate.

## 3. Editorial safety for first-person immigration stories

- Clearly distinguish personal experience, documents personally seen, and information from other sources.
- Attribute allegations to the speaker or to verifiable documents; do not present an unproven accusation against a named person or agency as an established fact.
- Do not publish passport numbers, case numbers, addresses, private contact details, or other identifying documents.
- Explain practical document checks without suggesting that a visa alone guarantees lawful entry, residence, or work rights.
- For legal or immigration rules, verify the current official government source and show the date checked.
- Preserve the author's supplied first-person draft unless the author explicitly requests editing.

## 4. Image checks

- [ ] Open the exact image URL and confirm it is not a placeholder or broken link.
- [ ] Confirm the image belongs to the publisher or is licensed/authorized.
- [ ] Caption describes only what is visible or otherwise confirmed.
- [ ] Never use a generic homepage URL as an image URL.
- [ ] Do not invent an image URL when the original file is unavailable.

## 5. Final pre-publish review

- [ ] Preview on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Open the permalink and confirm it resolves.
- [ ] Check spelling, links, headings, captions, and disclosure placement.
- [ ] Confirm the selected label and search description.
- [ ] Confirm the article is the intended final draft.
- [ ] Publisher performs the final review and publishes manually.
