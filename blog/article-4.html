Rome, Paris, and Lisbon: practical trip notes

May 2026 • By TRTT Editorial

Rome rewards travelers who plan around walking, early sightseeing, and strong food choices. It is a city where evenings matter just as much as daytime landmarks.

Paris benefits from a slower rhythm: focus on neighborhoods, cafés, and transit, rather than trying to “see everything” in a single day. Lisbon offers a mix of scenic city walks, good value, and easy day-trip opportunities.

Whether you choose one or all three, the most useful advice is to keep the daily plan realistic and leave room for spontaneous stops. That is what turns a busy trip into a memorable one.
