Top picks

Best cities to visit

  • Rome for history, food, and classic sightseeing.
  • Paris for iconic culture and elegant city planning.
  • Barcelona for beaches, design, and nightlife.
  • Lisbon for value, viewpoints, and relaxed city walking.
Planning

Smart trip preparation

  • Compare flights and rail routes early.
  • Pick one country or cluster of cities per trip.
  • Use local transit to save money and time.
  • Keep a daily budget buffer for surprises.
Essentials

Travel essentials

  • eSIM for mobile data and maps.
  • Travel insurance and emergency contact prep.
  • Portable charger and simple packing list.
  • Offline maps and local transport apps.
