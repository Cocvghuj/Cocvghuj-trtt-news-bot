How to build a realistic Europe budget

May 2026 • By TRTT Editorial

Budgeting for Europe is not just about adding a low price to flights. A realistic budget includes transport, food, local transit, emergency costs, and small pleasures that make a trip feel enjoyable.

Start by identifying your core route. If you are visiting two cities, estimate transport between them before booking. Then set a daily average for food and local movement. Many travelers underestimate daily cost in high-demand tourist areas.

The smartest approach is to keep one section of your budget flexible. That buffer prevents stress if a train changes, a hotel is pricier than expected, or you decide to splurge on one memorable experience.
