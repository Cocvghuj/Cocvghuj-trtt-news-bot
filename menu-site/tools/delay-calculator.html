🛡️ TRTT FLIGHT RIGHTS EU passenger rights
European Passenger Rights Law • EC 261/2004

Could your flight qualify for compensation?

Some passengers may qualify for compensation after a long arrival delay, cancellation or denied boarding. Eligibility depends on the route, operating airline, cause and circumstances. Amounts and claim deadlines vary.

Before you submit a claim

Keep your booking confirmation, boarding pass and written updates from the airline. Check the actual arrival delay, operating carrier, route, reason for disruption and the applicable national deadline. This page is an information guide, not a legal decision or eligibility calculator.
