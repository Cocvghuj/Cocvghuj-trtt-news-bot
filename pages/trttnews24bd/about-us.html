About TRTT Voyage

From Vision to Global Brand! Explore Beyond. Discover Everything.

TRTT Voyage is an independent digital publishing and product-building project founded by Engr. Abu Taher. It brings together practical travel information, verified news, AI-assisted learning, free web tools, and carefully scoped software experiments.

Our focus

  • News: factual, dated, source-led reporting with clear corrections when needed.
  • Travel: destination guides, trip-planning information, and useful resources.
  • AI: explainers and transparent, human-reviewed AI-assisted workflows.
  • Tools: practical web utilities and software experiments, with their limits explained.

Our working principles

  • Truth before claims; sources before publication.
  • Privacy and security before automation.
  • Preserve existing content and history.
  • Test features before describing them as working.
  • Disclose affiliate relationships clearly.
