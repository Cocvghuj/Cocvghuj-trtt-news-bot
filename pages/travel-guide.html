Europe Travel Guide

Planning a trip to Europe can be exciting, but it also means balancing budget, timing, local transport, and route planning. This guide gives you a simple and useful strategy for moving through Europe without unnecessary stress.

Best countries for first-time visitors

  • Italy for food, history, culture, and classic city breaks.
  • Spain for beaches, city life, and easy budget planning.
  • Portugal for affordable cities and scenic coastal routes.
  • France for iconic cities and classic European experiences.
  • Greece for island travel and warm-weather escapes.

Best time to visit

  • Spring and early autumn are usually best for comfortable weather and lower prices.
  • Summer is popular and often more expensive.
  • Winter works well for Christmas markets and mountain destinations.

Budget tips

  • Book transport early and compare flights and train routes.
  • Use central bases to reduce local transportation costs.
  • Use travel tools like eSIM, airport transfer bundles, and route planners.
  • Travel in shoulder seasons for cheaper hotels and fewer crowds.

Sample 7-day route

  1. Day 1–2: Rome
  2. Day 3: Florence
  3. Day 4–5: Venice or Milan
  4. Day 6: Lisbon or Barcelona
  5. Day 7: Final city or departure
