TRTT VOYAGE DIARY

A Three-Day Journey of Coding, Patience and Learning

Some projects teach us more than we expect. Our recent journey with the TRTT Voyage Blogger theme was one of them.

What started as a theme development task turned into three days of coding, checking, testing, troubleshooting and learning. There were exciting moments, unexpected errors and plenty of lessons along the way.

DAY ONE — THE EXCITEMENT OF BUILDING

We started with a clear vision: create a beautiful, lightweight and mobile-friendly Blogger theme for TRTT Voyage — Sanluri to World.

The plan included a modern homepage, a Thinking Room menu, six Bubbles Hub categories and a distinctive visual identity for our project.

As development progressed, XML structure and code checks gave us encouraging results. But one important lesson was still waiting for us.

DAY TWO — WHEN “PASS” MEETS REALITY

A theme can look correct in a code inspection and still encounter problems when uploaded to Blogger.

We experienced restoration errors and discovered that checking XML syntax is only one part of the process.

Blogger has its own theme requirements. A successful XML check does not automatically guarantee a successful Blogger restoration.

This taught us to separate code validation, platform compatibility and real-world testing.

DAY THREE — THE VALUE OF PATIENCE

The third day brought another important lesson: technical limitations must be communicated clearly.

Not every tool is always available, and not every test can be completed in every environment. It is better to explain a limitation honestly than to promise a result that has not been confirmed.

We also learned that a good development process needs clear checkpoints, careful verification and realistic expectations.

FIVE LESSONS FROM THREE DAYS

  1. XML validity matters: A well-formed XML document is an essential starting point.
  2. Platform testing matters: Blogger compatibility must be checked separately.
  3. Never confuse PASS with completion: A partial test is not a finished product.
  4. Communication matters: Explain what has been tested and what remains uncertain.
  5. Patience matters: Difficult technical problems are solved through careful work, not rushed promises.

OUR NEXT STEP

Our goal remains the same: a reliable Blogger theme that respects the existing TRTT Voyage content and delivers a smooth experience on mobile and desktop.

Before calling any future release final, we must review its XML structure, check Blogger-specific requirements and confirm restoration wherever actual testing is possible.

MORE THAN A THEME

TRTT Voyage is a journey built around creativity, technology and a shared vision.

Muse brings direction and ideas. Abu brings the publishing vision. ChatGPT contributes coding assistance, explanations and technical support.

Different roles, one project and one important principle: keep learning, keep improving and be honest about every result.

“PASS is a checkpoint, not the destination. Real success is when the work actually works.”

FINAL THOUGHT

Three days taught us something valuable: building a website is not only about writing code. It is also about testing, patience, communication and learning from mistakes.

We are moving forward with a clearer process and a better understanding of what a real Blogger theme needs.

The journey continues. TRTT Voyage — Sanluri to World.
