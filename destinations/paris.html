Destination

Rome, Italy

Rome rewards travelers who mix history, food, and walking-friendly neighborhoods with a realistic daily pace.

Best for

  • First-time Europe travelers
  • History lovers
  • Food-focused city breaks

Budget guide

  • Stay in central but not premium neighborhoods
  • Use public transport and walk where possible
  • Book museum entries in advance

Must-see

  • Colosseum and nearby historic core
  • Trevi Fountain and Spanish Steps
  • Trastevere for food and evening atmosphere
