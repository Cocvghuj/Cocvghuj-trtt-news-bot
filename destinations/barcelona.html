Destination

Paris, France

Paris works best when you balance iconic sights with neighborhood walks and a slower pace.

Best for

  • Art and culture
  • Classic city breaks
  • Food and café culture

Budget guide

  • Use metro passes strategically
  • Book museum tickets in advance
  • Limit major sightseeing to specific zones

Must-see

  • Eiffel Tower area
  • Louvre and Seine walks
  • Montmartre for atmosphere and views
