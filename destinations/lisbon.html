Destination

Barcelona, Spain

Barcelona combines beach energy, design-led neighborhoods, and easy city walking into a flexible travel experience.

Best for

  • Beach escapes
  • Food and nightlife
  • Walking-friendly city breaks

Budget guide

  • Use transport passes for city movement
  • Stick to a central base
  • Blend paid attractions with free walking routes

Must-see

  • Sagrada Família
  • Barceloneta Beach
  • Gothic Quarter
