Cabin bag size checker

Measure your packed bag, enter the cabin-bag limits shown for your airline and fare, and compare them before you leave for the airport.

Use your airline's current allowance. There is no single cabin-bag size that applies to every airline or ticket. Check your booking or the airline's official baggage page and enter those limits below.

1. Your bag

Measure the outside of the packed bag, including wheels, handles and other protrusions.

2. Allowance from your airline or booking

Enter the maximum dimensions exactly as provided. The checker compares the three sides in any orientation.

Leave both weight fields blank if your allowance does not specify a weight limit. If you enter one, enter the other too.

    This is a measurement aid, not an airline approval or guarantee of carriage. Allowances can vary by airline, route, fare, and booking; staff may measure bags at the airport. Always confirm the current rule with your airline.
