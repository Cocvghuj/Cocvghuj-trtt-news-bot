Warsaw, Poland: My First-Hand Travel Experience

By Abu Taher · Travel experience: 12 February 2024

On 12 February 2024, I looked back on my own experience of Warsaw and everyday life in Poland. These are personal observations from the places I visited and the services I used—not a claim that every traveller will have exactly the same experience.

A clean and comfortable city

One of my strongest impressions was that the places I visited in Poland felt clean and organised. During my time there, I generally felt comfortable walking around and exploring. I did not experience the kind of public disorder that would make me avoid the areas I visited. Every city has different neighbourhoods and situations, so I always recommend that travellers stay aware of their surroundings.

Everyday convenience: BLIK and Żabka

BLIK made everyday payments feel quick and straightforward when I used it. Żabka shops were also convenient for everyday purchases. Together, these services gave me a good impression of how simple routine tasks could be while travelling or living in Poland. Availability and payment options can vary by shop, bank and customer, so check the conditions that apply to you.

Public transport made a strong impression

Poland's transport network was one of the things I appreciated most. For the journeys I personally made, getting around by public transport felt easier than I had found it during my own time in Helsinki, Finland. That is my personal comparison, not a measured ranking of the two countries. Before travelling, check the current timetable, ticket rules and route for your exact journey.

Helpful people made the experience warmer

I remember several helpful and friendly interactions with local people. In particular, some Polish women I asked for assistance were kind and willing to help. Those moments made me feel welcome and remain part of my positive memories of Poland.

My personal takeaway

For me, Warsaw and Poland were memorable not only for their buildings and streets, but also for practical everyday details: convenient payments, nearby shops, public transport and kind people.
