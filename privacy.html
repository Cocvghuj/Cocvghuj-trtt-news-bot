We may use analytics tools and cookies to understand how visitors use the site and which destinations or travel topics are most useful. This information is used in aggregate, not to identify individuals in most cases.

Affiliate and Partner Links

Some links on this site may be affiliate or partner links. For example, travel tools and booking links may use a locked marker like marker=787950 and a sub_id such as guide_airalo_787950. If a user clicks and books through those links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to the user.

We may use third-party services for analytics or advertising support. These services may have their own privacy practices. We recommend reviewing their policies if you use those platforms.
