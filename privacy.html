Privacy Notice

This notice explains the privacy principles for TRTT Voyage web pages and project resources.

Important: This is a plain-language project notice, not a completed technical audit of every live website, Blogger setting, analytics property, hosting provider, or third-party integration. The actual services enabled on a particular page may vary.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on the page and services enabled, technical logs such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested URL, and timestamps may be processed by hosting or security providers. If analytics, cookies, embedded media, forms, or affiliate links are enabled, those services may process additional usage or referral information under their own policies.

2. Information you choose to share

If you contact TRTT Voyage through a social platform or submit information through a form, that information is processed by the platform or service involved. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, payment details, identity documents, or API credentials through public channels.

3. Affiliate and external links

When you follow a link to a travel provider, retailer, social network, or other external service, you leave the TRTT Voyage page. The destination provider controls its own cookies, tracking, data collection, and privacy practices.

4. Data minimization and security

TRTT Voyage aims not to request sensitive information unless a specific, documented feature requires it. Project credentials are kept out of public source files. No website can promise absolute security; use care when sharing information online.

5. Your choices and requests

You can limit cookies through your browser settings and review the privacy controls offered by the external services you use. For a privacy-related question, contact TRTT Voyage through an official public channel. Requests involving data held by third-party providers must be directed to those providers.

6. Updates

This notice should be reviewed whenever analytics, cookies, forms, hosting, advertising, or affiliate integrations change. The date of this repository revision does not certify that every live service has been audited.
