Europe Travel Guide 2026: Budget Tips, Itineraries, and Best Destinations

Planning a trip to Europe can be exciting but complex. This guide helps you choose the best cities, understand travel costs, and build a practical itinerary without overspending.

Best countries in Europe

  • Italy for food, history, and culture.
  • Spain for beaches, cities, and value.
  • Portugal for affordable travel and scenic coastlines.
  • France for iconic cities and classic experiences.
  • Greece for island travel and coastal escapes.

Best time to visit

  • Spring and early autumn are often the most comfortable.
  • Summer is busiest and more expensive.
  • Winter is ideal for Christmas markets and mountain destinations.

Budget travel tips

  • Book flights early for better prices.
  • Use trains for short and medium distances.
  • Stay in central neighborhoods for lower transport costs.
  • Compare eSIM, airport transfers, and hotel bundles.

Sample 7-day Europe itinerary

  1. Day 1–2: Arrive in Rome and explore the historic center.
  2. Day 3: Travel to Florence for art and local food.
  3. Day 4–5: Visit Venice or Milan depending on your route.
  4. Day 6: Explore a coastal city such as Lisbon or Barcelona.
  5. Day 7: Return or add one more destination based on your budget.

What to pack for Europe

  • Comfortable walking shoes.
  • Plug adapter for European outlets.
  • Lightweight jacket for cool evenings.
  • Portable charger and travel power bank.
  • Travel insurance details and emergency contact list.

How to choose the right trip

Choose a destination based on your priorities: culture, beaches, food, nightlife, hiking, or family travel. Europe is large, so it helps to group cities into one route to reduce transport costs and time.

When possible, compare flights, trains, and local transfer services before booking. Useful services such as travel insurance, eSIM packages, and airport transfer deals can reduce stress and save money.

  • eSIM services for mobile data without roaming charges.
  • Budget calculators for hotels, meals, and transport.
