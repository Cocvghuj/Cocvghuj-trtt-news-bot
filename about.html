Our Real Story • Qatar to Europe

About TRTT VoyageHub & News 24 BD

TRTT VoyageHub was founded with a singular purpose: to make real-world European travel practical, affordable, and transparent for everyday travelers and diaspora communities.

From Qatar Carriage Shifts to 21 Digital Tools

Back in 2020 during the height of the global pandemic, our founder managed carriage shifts on the ground in Qatar—wearing white shirt, hair cap, mask, gloves, with a handheld POS machine and stack of dispatch papers. That rigorous, hands-on logistics background proved that travel is not casual touring—it requires accurate routes, dependable data, fair compensation rights, and smart budget planning.

What We Provide

  • Unbiased Flight Comparison: Real-time airfare comparison across world airlines through verified partner tools.
  • Air Passenger Rights: Helping eligible travelers claim up to 600€ in cash compensation under European regulation EC 261/2004 via AirHelp.
  • Instant eSIM Solutions: Instant mobile 5G/4G connectivity across Europe without physical SIM swaps or extortionate roaming bills.
  • Fixed-Rate Airport Transfers: Eliminating taxi scams and metered surprises at key European transit gateways.
  • Curated Amazon Travel Gear: Verified airline-approved power banks, universal adapters, and durable travel gear for long journeys.

Our Core Principle: Super App Formula

An app without genuine community insights has tickets but no travelers. A blog without direct solutions provides information without execution. TRTT combines high-ranking travel guides with instant, secure partner booking capabilities in one unified ecosystem.
